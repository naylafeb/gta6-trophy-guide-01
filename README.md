Leonida Ledger — the best offline GTA VI trophy guide and tracker: all 55 official PS5 trophies (1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze) with their official names, descriptions and grades, a cited guide in our own words for every trophy, a 3-stage roadmap to the platinum (about 200 hours), a missable audit (only Positive Mental Attitude is potentially missable), and a tracker with localStorage progress, export/import, filters by kind and grade, and a spoiler setting that hides story trophies until earned. Sources consulted include the PowerPyx GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap, Exophase, PSNProfiles and GTABase. Works fully offline with no build step; no Rockstar logo, artwork or screenshots are used.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — or open `app/index.html` from any static server with `app/` at its root.
